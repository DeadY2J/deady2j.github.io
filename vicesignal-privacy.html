Privacy Policy

Vice Signal Uploader · Last updated: October 6, 2026

What this application is

"Vice Signal Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Vice Signal to upload the channel's own videos and to read the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

The application requests these Google permissions, always on the owner's own channel: youtube.upload (upload videos), youtube.readonly (read basic information about the channel and its videos) and yt-analytics.readonly (read the channel's own analytics reports). It does not access email, contacts, files or any other data from the Google account.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so that the tool can keep working without asking to log in again. Statistics read from the channel are used only by the owner to decide what to publish and are kept on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

For any question, contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel Vice Signal.
