Privacy Policy

The Late Hour Archive upload tool · Last updated: September 30, 2026

What this application is

"Canal Terror Youtube" is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Late Hour Archive to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

The application requests a single Google permission: youtube.upload, which allows it to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other data from the Google account, such as email, contacts, viewing history or private videos.

How data is stored

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so that the tool can keep uploading without asking to log in again. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

For any question, contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel @TheLateHourArchive.
